TETRIS
Roguelike — Field Guide

🎮 The Concept

Classic Tetris — but every level-up warps the rules. Clear lines to level up. At each level threshold the game freezes and forces you to choose one of three Modifiers: permanent rule changes that stack for the rest of your run. Some are blessings. Most are curses with score bonuses attached.

Survive to Level 15 to win. Dare to continue past victory and the game punishes you with 3 permanent lines and a harder level — but modifier picks keep coming.

📊 Scoring

Single: 100  ·  Double: 300  ·  Triple: 500  ·  Tetris: 800 — all × level
T-Spin Single / Double / Triple: 200 / 400 / 1600 × level
Back-to-Back: ×1.5 on qualifying clears (Tetris, T-Spins, All-Spins, PCs)
Combo: +50 × combo count × level per consecutive clear
Perfect Clear: +3000 × level  ·  Soft Drop: +1/row  ·  Hard Drop: +2/row

🧊 Special Cell Types

Hard-Frost Cells — From Frost Bag+. Near-white ice. Need 3 clears: first crack demotes to regular frost (vivid blue), second crack darkens to cracked frost (deep blue), third clear removes them.

Frost Cells — From Frost Bag. Vivid ice blue. Need 2 clears: first clear cracks them (deep steel blue), second clear removes them.

Cracked Frost — Any frost cell that has been cracked once. Deep steel blue with visible fracture lines. One more clear removes it.

Wound Lines — Inserted by Déjà Vu when you repeat the same clear type. Each wound row has a countdown (shown on the leftmost cell). It ticks down by lines cleared each turn; when it hits 0 the row erases itself entirely. They cannot be line-cleared directly.

Permanent Lines — Unbreakable grey rows added by Bedrock and post-win Continue. They sit at the very bottom, forever.

⚡ Modifiers

Bedrock — 2 perm lines, level −1
Blindfolded — No ghost, +2000 pts
Blindfolded+ — No ghost, board turns grey
O-Spin Meta — T-spins ×1.5, +1 O per bag
O-Spin Meta+ — All-spin enabled, +2 O per bag
All-In — B2B bonus ×2, non-B2B scores 0
No DAS — No auto-repeat, score ×1.75
Permafrost — No DAS + wall-snap, score ×4
Déjà Vu — Repeat clears add wound lines, ×1.5
Déjà Vu+ — Wounds last 20 lines, ×2.5
Blackout — Board hidden 5s/shown 1s, ×1.5
Blackout+ — Hidden 10s/shown 1s, ×3 total
Earthquake — Board shifts every 10 pieces, ×2
Minimalist — 1 next piece shown, ×1.25
Minimalist+ — 1 grey next piece, ×1.5
Amnesia — Hold hidden but usable, ×1.5
Amnesia+ — Hold fully disabled, ×2
Overdrive — +3 levels now, score ×0.5
Thin Bag — 1 type removed per bag, ×1.5
Thin Bag+ — 3 removed, +1 back, ×2.5
Mirror — 180° key flips piece instead, ×1.5
Frost Bag — ~Half the pieces per bag are fully frozen (2 clears each)
Frost Bag+ — Half frozen as before; half of those are hard-frozen (3 clears)
Trash Bag — 1 mino per bag is trash (brown). Clearing it injects a garbage line with holes at trash columns
Tetraple — Triples → B2B (800pts); Tetrises → non-B2B (500pts)
T-Tetramino — All I-pieces become T-pieces (incl. current bag), ×1.5
BTB Chain — B2B stack (cap 25) scales BTB bonus: ×1.25…×2.50
Flow Meter — Eval bar: qualifying +1, non-qualifying −1. Overflow→500pts, underflow→wound row
BTB Chain+ — ×1.35…×3.10 per tier; breaking adds wound rows per tier
